Chava Interiors combines European craftsmanship with a human-centred approach to design. My goal is to create homes that feel elegant, calm and refined — but also truly functional, intuitive and supportive of family life.

## 6. Founder Story — Narrative Version

I grew up close to furniture, craftsmanship and the practical side of creating homes. In my family-run company, I learned that a well-made kitchen is not only a product. It is a system that has to work every day: for cooking, cleaning, storing, hosting, organising and living.

Over time, I became more and more interested in the human side of design. I started to ask not only what looks beautiful, but what truly supports the people who use a space. What makes daily life easier? What creates calm? What helps a family function better? What makes a home feel respectful of its values?

This way of thinking naturally led me to design thinking and service design. I began to see interiors as living systems — shaped around routines, rituals, relationships and needs.

My passion for Jewish culture opened a new direction. I became fascinated by the way Jewish home life is connected to tradition, rhythm, family, hospitality and ritual. A kosher kitchen is not just a kitchen. A Shabbat table is not just a dining table. A library of seforim is not just storage. These are meaningful spaces that carry identity, continuity and daily practice.

Chava Interiors was created from this intersection: European furniture craftsmanship, human-centred design and deep respect for Jewish life.

## 9. Design Philosophy

### Human-Centred Design

Every project begins with people, not furniture.

Chava Interiors looks at how a family lives: how they cook, host, celebrate, prepare for Shabbat, store seasonal items, use the kitchen during holidays, organise dishes, gather around the table and create a sense of home.

### Design Thinking

Design thinking helps translate real needs into practical solutions. Instead of assuming what a client needs, Chava Interiors listens, observes, maps routines and creates solutions that respond to daily life.

### Service Design

Service design means that the experience of the client matters as much as the final furniture. The process should be clear, respectful, organised and comfortable from the first conversation to the final installation.

### European Craftsmanship

The brand is rooted in bespoke furniture production and European craftsmanship. Materials, details and durability matter. Chava Interiors creates furniture that is made to serve the home for years.

## 10. Design Approach — Step by Step

1. **Listen**  
   Understand the family, lifestyle, religious practices, routines, storage needs and aesthetic preferences.

2. **Map the rituals**  
   Identify how the home functions during weekdays, Shabbat, holidays, hosting, cooking and family gatherings.

3. **Translate needs into zones**  
   Define kosher kitchen zones, storage systems, preparation areas, dining flow, children’s needs and guest hosting areas.

4. **Design the furniture system**  
   Create bespoke kitchens, dining furniture, libraries, wardrobes and storage solutions that support real use.

5. **Refine materials and details**  
   Choose finishes, colours, hardware, lighting and proportions that create a premium, calm and timeless interior.

6. **Coordinate production**  
   Use the experience of a family-run furniture company to ensure quality, precision and durability.

7. **Deliver a home that works**  
   The final goal is not only visual beauty, but a home that feels natural, ordered and supportive.
